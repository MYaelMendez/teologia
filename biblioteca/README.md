# 🏵️ Biblioteca Luterana

**Fuentes preservadas. Ediciones identificadas. Enseñanza compartida.**

Biblioteca Luterana is the curated, multilingual reference collection of **TEOLOGIA · διδαχή**, developed for the Latin American Lutheran Institute. It is a bibliographic and educational infrastructure, not an indiscriminate PDF dump.

**Navegación:** [Español](README.es.md) · [English](README.en.md) · [Português](README.pt-BR.md)

## Collections

- **Escritura / Scripture** — editions, biblical languages, references and study aids
- **Confesiones / Confessions** — Book of Concord and Lutheran confessional documents
- **Catecismos / Catechisms** — Luther's Small and Large Catechisms, with the [Enchiridion learning pathway](../content/enchiridion/README.md)
- **Historia / History** — Reformation and Latin American Lutheran mission history
- **Teología / Theology** — research, theological education and reviewed commentary
- **Liturgia e himnología / Liturgy and hymnody** — worship resources and music bibliography
- **Archivo LATAM / Latin American archive** — oral histories, educational projects, and institutional records, only with appropriate permissions

## Catalog

The initial [catalog.json](catalog.json) provides a versioned schema and starter bibliographic records. **A record is not a claim that a digital edition is hosted or legally reusable.** Consult the original publisher, rights holder and authoritative edition.

## Editorial policy

Every catalog record identifies its work, author, language, edition/translation where known, provenance, rights status, and review status. Cite precise editions and stable external links; do not copy restricted texts, scans, or student information. Keep original sources distinct from translations, teaching notes and AI-generated summaries. All doctrinal and historical annotations require human review before approval.

## Future reader

The library will support search, filters, citations, 9:16 reading, accessible text, and student-forked bibliographies. These are planned features, not yet deployed.
