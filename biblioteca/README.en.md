# 🏵️ Lutheran Library · Biblioteca Luterana

**Preserve · Study · Share · Teach.**

The Lutheran Library is the multilingual bibliographic collection of TEOLOGIA · διδαχή for the Latin American Lutheran Institute.

Collection pathways cover Scripture, Lutheran confessions, catechisms, history, theology, liturgy, hymnody, and Latin American archives. Each resource must identify its work, author, edition, language, provenance, rights and review status.

Copyrighted works are not automatically mirrored. Translations, commentary and AI-assisted materials remain distinguishable from primary sources and require human review.
